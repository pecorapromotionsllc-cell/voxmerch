# voxmerch-launch-ops

The launch project management itself. Monday.com integration, launch checklists, timelines, the five-board workspace. The "behind the scenes" binder for running the launch as a project.

## Current contents

- `Vox Merch Launch Action Plan - April to June 2026.docx` — active launch plan (60KB, Apr 5 version).
- `voxmerch-daily-brief/SKILL.md` — Cowork scheduled task that produces the daily brief.
- `external-skills/business-coach/` — strategy/decision skill for launch-window calls. See `../docs/external-skills.md`.

## What goes here
Launch checklists, go/no-go criteria, cross-functional timelines, Monday.com exports, scheduled task definitions, post-launch retro notes.

## What does NOT go here
Marketing content produced during the launch (→ `voxmerch-content/`), sales collateral (→ `voxmerch-sales/`).
