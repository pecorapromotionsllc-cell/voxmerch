# Brand Context

## Company Name
Vox Merch

## About
Vox Merch delivers premium trade show merchandise and brand activations for enterprise clients and partners. The company blends advanced technology with human creativity to produce show-floor-ready brand experiences — from real-time fulfillment platforms to curated activation merchandise.

## Industry
Trade show activations, premium branded merchandise, enterprise brand experiences, live events

## Target Audience
- Enterprise marketing and events teams responsible for trade show presence
- Brand managers and procurement leads at mid-market and enterprise companies
- Trade show and conference sponsors planning activations at major industry events (CES, Dreamforce, etc.)

## Known Brand Materials
- Brand guidelines: .claude/brand-voice-guidelines.md (in this workspace)

## Additional Context
Vox Merch operates at the intersection of technology and live experience. The brand's primary differentiator is deep trade show expertise combined with a technology-driven fulfillment platform — meaning clients avoid the common Day 1 logistics chaos that plagues most exhibitors. The brand voice should always signal insider knowledge of the show floor, a premium standard of quality, and an outcome-first approach that avoids jargon.

Key terms to use: activation, show floor, brand experience, premium, partner, fulfillment.
Terms to avoid: swag, solutions, seamless, leverage, revolutionary, game-changing, world-class, cutting-edge.
