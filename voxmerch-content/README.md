# voxmerch-content

All written marketing. Social captions, LinkedIn posts, email sequences, blog drafts, the pre-launch content calendar, ad copy. Words, not code.

## Current contents

- `event-activations-are-having-a-moment-blog.md` — blog draft.
- `AI-Social-Media-Manager/` — your Claude Code project for AI-assisted social media (CLAUDE.md, content-plan.md, post-log.md, images, local `.claude/` settings).
- `external-skills/marketingskills-main/` — broad marketing skill pack (copywriting, ad creative, email sequences, CRO). See `../docs/external-skills.md`.

## What goes here
Blog posts, email flows, LinkedIn and Twitter drafts, ad copy, content calendars, newsletters, press releases.

## What does NOT go here
Sales decks and outreach (→ `voxmerch-sales/`), website copy that lives on marketing pages (→ `voxmerch-marketing-site/`).
