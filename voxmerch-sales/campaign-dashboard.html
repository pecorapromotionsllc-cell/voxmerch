The six gates

Each gate feeds the next, so a stall low on the ladder makes everything above it unreadable. The diagnosis column is the part worth keeping: it says what a miss at that specific gate means, which is not the same answer as "send more."

01

Sends actually leaving the building

The old campaign died here: 35 emails in its entire life against a 1,550/day ceiling, because two approval gates required a founder who was busy closing to also be the send button. Both gates are gone. If this number stops moving, nothing else on this page means anything. Throughput is fine. An earlier read of this dashboard warned that sending was capped near 7/day and might need a second mailbox. That was wrong: Apollo's delivered counter lags by hours. All 25 went out within about 27 hours, confirmed one by one in the Sent Items folder. No second mailbox needed. Email 2 now proven at scale, not just on a cohort of four. The 30 July cohort of four all received email 2 on 4 Aug between 10:02 and 10:33am CT. The remaining 21, who got email 1 on Friday 31 July, came due Wednesday 5 Aug: 20 of the 21 went out, paced from 10:01am to 3:08pm CT, counted one by one in Sent Items. The 21st is Emma Jack at Legacy Marketing, still sitting at step 2 in the pacing queue rather than failed. Batch 2 was enrolled the same afternoon: 49 contacts, 25 active immediately and 24 staged paused to auto-unpause the next morning, because 49 in one window would have doubled the largest send day so far. The three-day gap counts business days: Thursday plus three business days is Friday, Monday, Tuesday. Two earlier readings of this dashboard were wrong and are worth recording so the mistake is not repeated. The 3 Aug check assumed calendar days. The 4 Aug 10am check ran minutes before the first send, then read an open send window with no sends as proof of failure and reached a confident wrong diagnosis. Apollo does not flush a step when the window opens, it paces roughly ten minutes apart across the day, so an empty Sent Items in the morning means nothing. A per-step max_emails_per_day set on step 1 but absent on steps 2 and 3 looked like the cause and was not: step 2 sends fine without it. No sequence edit was made.

04

Pitch Kits accepted

The real month-one test, and the one number worth losing sleep over. A partner has to name a live client and a dated event to accept, so everyone who says yes is in-market by definition. Under 5 by 31 August means the offer is wrong: stop scaling and rebuild it rather than buying more volume against a proposition that is not landing.

Qualified conversations

Kits accepted but conversations not converting points at price or tier fit, not the message. The qualifying question that sets the tier is one sentence: how many days, and how many kiosks running each day? A two-day show with two kiosks is 600 capacity, which is Advanced.
