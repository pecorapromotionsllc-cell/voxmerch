# voxmerch-sales

Outreach and sales materials for the events channel: experiential agencies, event activation companies, and resale-capable planner firms. Decks, one-pagers, demo scripts, follow-up templates, the Apollo sequence spec, and the pipeline reconciler. Open this binder when you are actively prospecting.

**Channel scope, as of 2026-09-24.** VoxMerch sells through the events channel only. There is no branded merchandise or promotional products distributor track, deferred or otherwise. Earlier documents in this folder that say distributor outreach is "deferred to January 1" are superseded; that date is not a plan. The one exception is HALO distributors who have already presented VoxMerch to their clients, and only those.

## Current contents

- `outbound-plan-aug-oct-2026.md`, the campaign plan and the decisions log behind it.
- `apollo-sequence-rebuild-2026-08.md`, spec for the live Event Agencies and Planners 3-Touch sequence.
- `audience-tranche-1-2026-08.md`, the first audience cut and how it was filtered.
- `pipeline-status-reference.md`, what each Sequence Stage and board group means.
- `pitch-kit-template.md`, partner-facing pitch kit template.
- `campaign-dashboard.html`, source for the live pipeline dashboard artifact.
- `scripts/stage_sync.py`, the Apollo to Monday Sequence Stage reconciler run by the weekday routine.
- `VoxMerch_FAQs_ORIGINAL_2026-08-01.txt` and `VoxMerch_FAQs_CORRECTED_pending_upload.txt`, FAQ copy before and after correction.
- `VoxMerch_Licensed_Client_SOP.docx`, Licensed Client standard operating procedure.

## What goes here
Cold email sequences, call scripts, demo scripts, one-pagers, sales decks, objection-handling docs, battle cards, SOPs for licensed clients and distributors.

## What does NOT go here
Blog and social content (→ `voxmerch-content/`), launch project management (→ `voxmerch-launch-ops/`).
